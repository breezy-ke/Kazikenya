## Full description (English)

Need a plumber in Kasarani, a mama fua in Nyali or a web designer anywhere in Kenya? KaziKenya connects you with trusted, rated pros in all 47 counties — and your money stays safe until the job is done.

**How it works**
• Post a job free and get quotes from pros near you — often within the hour
• Or browse verified profiles, ratings and work photos and book directly
• Pay with Lipa na M-Pesa into escrow. The pro sees the payment is secured
• Confirm the job is done and the money is released. Problem? Report it and our team steps in

**Why people trust KaziKenya**
✓ ID-verified and Pro-verified badges
✓ Honest ratings and reviews from real bookings
✓ M-Pesa escrow — never pay for work that wasn't done
✓ Phone numbers shared only after payment is secured
✓ Full Kiswahili support and dark mode
✓ Light on data and fast on any Android phone

**For fundis and freelancers**
Get job alerts near you, send quotes, chat with clients and get paid straight to your M-Pesa. Free to join. Founding pros get 3 months of KaziKenya Pro free.

Services: plumbing, electrical, cleaning, mama fua, house help, carpentry, masonry & tiling, painting, car mechanics, appliance & phone repair, salon & beauty, tailoring, moving, gardening, welding, solar & CCTV, fumigation, tutoring, catering, events & DJ, photography, drivers, security — plus remote web/app development, graphic design, writing & translation, digital marketing, video editing, accounting & KRA returns and virtual assistants.

Made in Kenya, for Kenya 🇰🇪
